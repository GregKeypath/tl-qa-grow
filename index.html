GROW Model Reference

Use this as a guide during every coaching session
G
Goal
Set the intention

Agree on what a good outcome looks like for both of you before looking at the call.

  • What would you like to get out of today?
  • What does a great call look like to you?
  • By the end, what would make this feel worthwhile?
R
Reality
Explore the situation

Listen to their perspective before sharing any evaluation data. Their view first.

  • How do you feel the call went overall?
  • Was there a moment where things shifted?
  • If you could change one thing, what would it be?
O
Options
Generate ideas together

Let the advisor lead on solutions. Your role is to ask, not prescribe. One specific change beats a long list.

  • What could you do differently to achieve that?
  • What has worked for you in a similar situation?
  • On a scale of 1 to 10, how confident are you that would work?
W
Way Forward
Lock in the commitment

One or two focused actions is better than a long list. The commitment must be audible in the next recording.

  • What is the one thing you will do differently?
  • How will we know it has happened?
  • When shall we check in on this?
