Evaluation Groups and Weightings

Group Focus Weight
Compliance Call recorded + student ID confirmed Auto-Fail
A: Connect Opening, listening, communication throughout 10%
B: Explore Effective questioning, confirming understanding 40%
C: Quality of Advice Appropriate advice, presenting options 30%
D: Resolve Clear close, confirmed next steps, student confidence 20%
Compliance Fail: Auto-fails the entire evaluation regardless of other scores. Coaching debrief within 48 hours. Manager notified.

Acknowledging — Walk Your SSA Through This

1
Open the evaluation from the Inbox notification or Evaluations tab
2
Read through all scores and evaluator comments
3
Listen to the call recording using the link at the top
4
Click Acknowledge at the bottom of the evaluation
Key message for SSAs: Acknowledging does not mean you agree. It confirms you have read the evaluation. You can still dispute after acknowledging.

Raising a Dispute

1
Open the evaluation and click Dispute
2
Type the reason — be specific, reference the question and the call moment
3
Click Submit — status changes to In Dispute
4
TL reviews and responds. SSA notified of the outcome
Disputes should be specific, professional, and prompt. Do not wait more than a few days after release.

Evaluation Statuses

Released Evaluation is visible. SSA has not yet acknowledged it.
Acknowledged SSA has read and confirmed receipt.
In Dispute SSA has raised a dispute. TL is reviewing.
Dispute Accepted Score updated following a successful dispute.
Dispute Rejected Original score upheld. Reason provided by TL.

Key Reminders for TLs

  • Two evaluations per advisor per month are scheduled
  • Release the evaluation before or during the GROW coaching session
  • Evaluator comments are visible to the SSA — write them accordingly
  • N/A on Q6 only when no options genuinely existed
  • Compliance fail requires manager notification within 48 hours
  • Score 85% and above — consider using the call in calibration
  • Score below 60% — coaching debrief and loop in your manager
